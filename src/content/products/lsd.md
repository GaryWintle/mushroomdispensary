LSD — lysergic acid diethylamide — is a classic psychedelic used therapeutically for mood, creativity, and personal insight. We carry it in two formats to suit different needs and experience levels.

**Liquid LSD** comes in amber dropper bottles containing 240µg dissolved in 30ml of distilled water. Each 1ml dropper delivers 8µg — a standard microdose. The dropper is marked at 0.5ml and 1ml for easy, precise dosing. Store in a cool, dark place for best shelf life.

**Paper tabs** are available in-store for those who prefer a more traditional format. Tabs are suitable for both microdose and full psychedelic-level experiences depending on the dose taken.

A typical microdose is 8–16µg. A typical full psychedelic dose is around 100µg. We recommend the same schedule as mushroom microdosing: one dose every three days, or twice a week.
