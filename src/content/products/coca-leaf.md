Coca leaf is the raw plant from which cocaine is derived, but the two are not equivalent. Chewing or brewing whole coca leaves produces mild stimulant effects — gentle energy, reduced appetite, and altitude sickness relief — without the intense or addictive properties of its processed derivative.

Coca has been used for thousands of years by Indigenous peoples of the Andes for practical and ceremonial purposes. It is a daily part of life in many Andean communities, where it is chewed, brewed as tea, or used in ritual contexts.

We carry coca leaf in two forms:

- **Whole leaf** — for traditional chewing or brewing as tea
- **Powdered** — for tea or other preparations
