We carry a rotating selection of dried psilocybe cubensis mushrooms, from approachable beginner strains to more potent exotic varieties. All dried mushrooms can be eaten directly, brewed as tea, or blended into food like chocolate or peanut butter.

Our current strains include:

- **Golden Teacher** — warm, introspective, and reliable. Recommended for first-time users
- **Transkei** — visually vivid with geometric patterns and heightened senses
- **McKenna** — one of the most potent varieties, for experienced users only

We also carry **vegan chocolates** containing 1g of dried mushroom per piece, and **gummies** — a convenient, precisely dosed alternative to raw mushrooms.

More exotic strains and formats are available at our storefront locations than by mail order. Ask our staff about what's currently in stock.
