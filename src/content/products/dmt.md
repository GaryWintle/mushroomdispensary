DMT — dimethyltryptamine — is one of the most potent psychedelics known. Effects come on within 30 seconds of inhalation, peak rapidly, and typically resolve within 15–20 minutes, making it one of the shortest-acting but most intense psychedelic experiences available.

We carry two forms of NN-DMT:

**Vape cartridges** contain synthetically produced, chemically pure NN-DMT in propylene glycol and vegetable glycerin — the same base as most THC and nicotine vape pens. Available in two sizes:

- **Small (0.5ml)** — 300mg, approximately 10 strong doses
- **Large (1ml)** — 700mg, approximately 23 strong doses

**DMT powder** is 500mg of pure NN-DMT for experienced users who prefer direct vaporisation. Requires a suitable glass pipe. The powder should never be exposed to direct flame.

Set your vape pen to its highest heat setting. Always start with a short inhalation and wait before taking more.
