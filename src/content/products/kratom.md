The powdered leaf of a tree native to Southeast Asia, kratom has opiate-like properties and is used for pain relief, sedation, relaxation, and euphoria.

It comes in three main varieties:

- **Red** — sedating and pain-relieving
- **White** — stimulating and energising
- **Green** — balanced between the two

_Available in-store as powder and capsules._
