Peyote is a small, slow-growing cactus native to Mexico and the southern United States. It is one of the oldest known psychedelic medicines, with a history of ceremonial and healing use among Indigenous peoples of the Americas stretching back thousands of years. Its primary active compound is mescaline.

The experience is long-lasting — typically 8 to 12 hours — and tends to be deeply introspective, visually rich, and emotionally significant. Many users describe a strong sense of connection to nature, community, and self.

We carry peyote in two forms:

- **Dried peyote buttons** — for traditional oral use
- **Live cactus plants** — a living specimen you can grow at home. Some larger plants can fetch over $700 depending on age and size

We also carry **San Pedro**, a related mescaline-containing cactus that is generally considered more approachable and easier to source. Ask in-store about current availability.
