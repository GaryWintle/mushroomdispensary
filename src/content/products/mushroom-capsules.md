Our psilocybe cubensis capsules offer a convenient and precisely measured way to microdose or take a controlled therapeutic dose. All capsules are made with Golden Teacher mushroom and are available in two bases — spirulina in a standard pharmaceutical capsule, or oat flour in a vegan capsule. Both are gluten-free.

Capsules are available in a range of strengths:

- **12.5mg** — entry-level, ideal for sensitive individuals
- **25mg** — a gentle starting point for most people
- **50mg** — standard therapeutic microdose range
- **100mg** — higher end of the microdose range
- **500mg** — moderate psychedelic dose, for experienced users

Most people find benefits at 75–150mg taken twice a week for microdosing purposes. A compassionate price discount is available upon request.
